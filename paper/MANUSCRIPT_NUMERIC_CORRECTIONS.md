# Numeric corrections to the paper-1 manuscript, 2026-10-29

Two numerical errors were found while building the progress-report deck, whose
values are extracted from the frozen result files by script rather than copied
from the manuscript. Both are corrected here. Neither affects any frozen
artifact, any evaluation, or any conclusion: no result was recomputed, no
threshold or model was touched, and the 30-person cohort was not reopened.

The editing surface is the four section drafts; `paper/merge_manuscript.py`
regenerates `PAIR_SV_v1_manuscript.md` from them. The Russian manuscript and
the plain-language explainer are separate texts and were edited directly.

---

## Correction 1 — read-branch overstatement ratio: 10.4x → 10.5x

**What was wrong.** §10.3 and §15.6 reported the read branch's zero-ablation
overstatement as 10.4x. That value was obtained by dividing the two *rounded*
numbers printed in the §10.3 table (0.00751 / 0.00072 = 10.43). Computed from
the source values it is 10.498, i.e. 10.5x.

**Why it mattered.** Figure 4 computes the ratio from the source files and
annotates 10.5x, so the manuscript text and its own figure disagreed. The other
two branches are unaffected: candidate 5.095 → 5.1x and scalar 6.627 → 6.6x
round identically either way.

**Source values** (`reports/train40/ablations_aggregate.csv`,
`reports/train40/three_seed_aggregate.csv`):

| Branch | Zero-ablation ΔF1 | Retrained-removal ΔF1 | Ratio | Reported before | Reported now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Candidate | −0.005435 | −0.001067 | 5.095 | 5.1x | 5.1x (unchanged) |
| Read | −0.007511 | −0.000715 | **10.498** | **10.4x** | **10.5x** |
| Scalar | −0.010021 | −0.001512 | 6.627 | 6.6x | 6.6x (unchanged) |

**Deliberately left as written.** The summary range "5-10x" is kept. It is
hedged as "roughly" or "~" wherever it carries a claim, and 10.5 is within
rounding of its upper end. Changing the headline phrase everywhere for a
difference of 0.5 would have risked new inconsistencies across two languages
for no gain in accuracy.

## Correction 2 — what M4's HG002 shortfall is measured against: 2.2 → 1.7 points

**What was wrong.** Five sentences stated that M4 "fell 2.2 points below" the
95% recall target on HG002. The number 2.2 is correct, but it is the gap
between M2 and M4, not the gap between M4 and the target.

- M2 recall 0.955074, M4 recall 0.932728
- 0.95 − 0.932728 = 0.017272 → **1.7 points below the target**
- 0.955074 − 0.932728 = 0.022346 → 2.2 points below M2

**Why it survived earlier review.** `WEEK4_FINAL_PRE_SUBMISSION_AUDIT.md`
checked this claim and recorded "2.2 vs 2.23 — OK". The audit verified that the
*number* matched a real quantity; it did not verify that the quantity was the
one the sentence named. That file is a historical record of what was checked at
the time and is left unmodified.

**Fix applied.** Every occurrence keeps the target as the referent and takes the
correct value, 1.7. The M2-vs-M4 distance is already stated separately and
correctly elsewhere, so nothing was lost.
